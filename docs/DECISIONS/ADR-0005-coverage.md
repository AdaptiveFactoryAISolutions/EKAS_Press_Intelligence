# ADR-0005 — Pairwise observability discrimination

Status: Implemented architectural decision; milestone acceptance remains separate.

## Decision

Compute explainable fault-class pairs and unresolved equivalence from required and discriminating observations using public domain definitions.

## Reason

A raw percentage cannot explain same-symptom ambiguity. Available observations must identify which candidate pairs remain unresolved.

## Consequences and limits

Formal catalog rules are engineering priors and require plant qualification; they are not statistical proof of universal identifiability.
