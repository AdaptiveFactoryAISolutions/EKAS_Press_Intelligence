# Evidence-governed RCA

The standard-library-only engine imports public catalog priors, never a simulator or truth store. It receives samples and recorded operating context. Questions cannot select a physical answer. It compares first/last quartile means and variation inside the requested evidence window and evaluates explicit multivariate mechanism rules. Each catalog class receives supporting, contradicting and missing observations plus a governed disposition.

Data quality excludes invalid observations. MISSING/STUCK acquisition flags and recorded operator/planned actions take priority over equipment hypotheses. Ambient correlation has no causal rule. Maintenance records are contextual provenance and cannot determine a diagnosis. Catalog item/mechanism language describes a hypothesis, not a verified physical finding.

The engine never emits VERIFIED_ROOT_CAUSE. Multiple positively supported causes remain alternatives. Sparse observations preserve missing evidence. EVIDENCE_SUPPORTED_CAUSE requires multiple measured mechanism observations and no other positively supported cause; independent physical verification remains required. Confidence labels are evidence qualifications rather than calibrated probabilities.

Known limits: quartile comparison can miss short events or recovered excursions in a long window; overlapping episodes need bounded investigation windows. Rules are engineering priors, not a learned causal model. Historical maintenance is retained but not causally scored. No synthetic benchmark result establishes plant readiness.

The caller may provide `baseline_samples` independently of the selected analysis window. Counters are compared as increments per elapsed second, not raw accumulated totals. Four-percent tail trimming suppresses isolated analog spikes; sustained excursions, variance and robust window peaks retain mechanism evidence. Provenance records include the statistics and trimming fraction. Recorded operator context and historian quality have their own evidence IDs. Supporting hypotheses reference those IDs rather than hidden event identifiers.

Retained tests exercise all 16 isolated enhanced scenarios using public samples plus baseline, and verify sparse starvation remains unobservable, wording cannot inject causes, artifacts/benign actions do not establish physical failures, ambient correlation does not choose a cause, and isolated spikes do not establish physical failures. This is synthetic rule validation, not calibrated diagnostic accuracy across arbitrary plants or compound scenarios.

Complete mechanism signatures are conjunctive. A matching downstream stop, current variation or feed loss cannot by itself support every upstream cause. Known contradictory mechanism observations eliminate a candidate; any missing essential observation instead preserves NOT_OBSERVABLE. Material-starvation rules require stock loss, buffer depletion and an available decoiler; repeated-feed interruption requires stable stock and pressure so buffer depletion is not relabeled as a relay defect. Quality-only deterioration is contrasted with the load rise required for die wear.

A physical cause becomes eligible for an item/mechanism claim only when its full signature is supported, no other complete signature is supported, and no catalog competitor remains unobservable or unresolved. Two independent observations are required except position-sensor drift, whose public discriminator is a persistent position trend with stable short-term residual variance. This exception qualifies an evidence-supported sensor hypothesis, never external physical verification. The response exposes `cause_claim_eligible` separately from the diagnosis state.

A full-campaign regression exposed a distinction from isolated cases: partial matches had retained downstream symptom hypotheses, and secondary contradictory observations had incorrectly removed candidates whose essential sensors were missing. Both behaviors have regression tests and now use the conjunctive/missing-evidence rules above. Initial campaign results are retained by the validation workflow rather than overwritten as if they had passed.

Maintenance repeat records, PFMEA and control-plan entries receive separate CONTEXTUAL evidence records. Hypotheses link records by explicit asset; asset-free process priors remain labeled unscoped. These links never change measured mechanism scores or eliminate contradictions. Reasons enumerate the actual supporting, contradicting or missing observations rather than asserting that a work order or prevention plan proves a cause.

Quality-inspection records provide an alternate measured source when the selected historian profile lacks the raw quality signal. The engine compares their timestamped inspection measurements and counter increments using matching baseline times; provenance identifies `quality_inspection` and `inspection-*` references. It does not synthesize an unmeasured tonnage signal or eliminate die wear from quality trend alone. Sparse quality degradation therefore preserves the missing load discriminator. Raw historian quality, when available, remains the primary source without double-counting inspection observations.
