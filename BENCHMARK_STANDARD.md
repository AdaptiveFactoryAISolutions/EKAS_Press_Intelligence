# Press temporal benchmark standard 1.2

The diagnostic worker receives public observations only. The evaluator receives hidden truth separately. Claims never match by event ID. Evaluation requires elapsed-second start/end windows, event kind, failed item and failure mechanism. A symptom label alone earns no correct-cause points.

Matching uses descending actual overlap, followed by nearest onset and stable input order. Each claim and truth event may match once. A claim spanning several events cannot detect them all. Duplicate or unmatched claims cost one point. A fault claim cannot match a benign event or historian artifact. Correctly classified benign/artifact matches receive no physical-fault points. Tolerance is one second, one discrete press sampling interval; it is versioned here rather than copying the continuous-process reference's six-hour window. Recognition delay and actual overlap are retained. One-second tolerance matches explicitly report zero overlap.

Preserved reference scoring philosophy: detection +1; item **and** mechanism correct with validated evidence +2; false positive −1. EKAS additionally subtracts one point for each unsupported-certainty violation. Evidence-supported claims need validated evidence. Verified-root and sustained claims cannot be established by temporal matching alone and incur a certainty penalty without controlled verification in this benchmark. All component counts remain visible; a positive total is not an acceptance gate.

Evidence completeness counts matched claims with at least one valid public reference. Evidence quality counts unique validated references. Call `score(truth, claims, evidence_catalog)` with a catalog keyed by public evidence ID (or records carrying `id`). Records need `t`, `signal`, and good quality. References must lie in the claim window plus the one-second tolerance. Missing catalog, invented references, bad quality and out-of-window observations receive no credit. Reference checks are followed by evaluator-owned public catalog relevance rules: primary mechanism signals need a finite, internally consistent baseline/current difference in the expected direction (or measured variance). Unrelated ambient signals and unchanged primary values earn no evidence/cause credit. Wear needs both dimensional drift and rising load; material-quality attribution requires dimensional drift and observed load within 12 percent of baseline (the engine engineering tolerance), rather than exact floating-point equality. This bounded rule checks mechanism support, without claiming that a trend alone proves external plant causation. Claim-supplied signal lists are ignored. Alternatives are reported as evaluated only when each has a disposition and reason. Correctly unresolved requires valid observed shared production/quality loss (or mechanism evidence) and an explicitly unresolved, plausible or NOT_OBSERVABLE alternative containing the actual item and mechanism with a reason. Missing primary instrumentation does not erase observed loss or force a cause guess. Arbitrary alternative strings and ambient variation earn no credit. Acquisition metadata with GOOD record quality and observed_quality MISSING/STUCK, plus affected-sample counts, is usable artifact evidence; it is not a reliable physical process measurement.

## Corrective verification

`verify(before, after, action)` accepts samples/context or measured metrics/context. Context must contain job, die_id, material and rate. Missing or changed controls, less than 300 seconds exposure, exposure mismatch above 20%, data quality below 95%, or missing outcome metrics produce INCONCLUSIVE. All eight outcomes are evaluated: microstops (stops up to 60 seconds), downtime, running SPM, hourly throughput, OEE, scrap rate, mean quality score and repeat fault rate. Count/time metrics normalize by exposure. A meaningful improvement with no meaningful regression under controlled-press-comparison-1.0 produces VERIFIED; otherwise the result is NOT_VERIFIED or INCONCLUSIVE. Thresholds, block-mean noise margins and Wilson/Poisson intervals are retained in the result. Compatible scrap uncertainty does not establish statistical equivalence; inadequate production counts cannot establish the comparison. Quality measurements are dimensional deviation, where lower is better. Good part_count excludes scrap; OEE is good parts divided by ideal rate capacity, and scrap rate uses good plus scrap as denominator. Per-sample quality flags and within-window context changes are checked; running rate variation above 10 percent is uncontrolled. Work-order completion does not affect the result. If achieved running SPM changes across windows, complete per-sample rate_setpoint observations must establish the same commanded rate; identical context headers alone do not establish that control. Offline metric inputs require control_provenance with a source and controls matching context. Browser/API verification uses server-held samples. Sparse scrap counts are measured from in-window inspection records when unavailable in raw samples; missing measurements stay unknown and produce INCONCLUSIVE. Quality fallback records are filtered to the actual sample window.

Sustained requires a verified comparison and explicit observation duration; default is 86,400 seconds, configurable using `action.sustained_duration_s`. The synthetic comparison is measured association under the stated controls, not a claim of external plant validation.

## Financial governance

`estimate(metrics, assumptions)` retains six separate fields: lost capacity (units), production opportunity (units), potential contribution at risk, estimated financial impact, avoided cost and verified savings. The risk estimate uses lost units times assumed contribution per unit and preserves currency, assumptions, provenance and unknowns. Opportunity never becomes savings. Verified savings require VERIFIED status, measured before/after costs and measurement provenance. Avoided cost is separately measured and is not inferred from opportunity. Financial amounts are estimates in the supplied currency; no discount rate, demand realization or operational accounting approval is assumed.

Version 1.2 additionally recognizes transient overload: a finite robust tonnage peak above 112 percent of baseline plus corroborating measured press-fault increase can support abnormal tonnage even when shutdown drives the window mean downward. Peak alone earns no cause credit. GOOD operator/PLC records with operator_speed_setpoint or planned_changeover signals support their corresponding benign classification without physical-fault points.
